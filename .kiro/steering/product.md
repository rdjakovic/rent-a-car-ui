# Product Overview

NextStep Rent-a-Car is a modern car rental management system with a React frontend and Spring Boot backend. The application provides comprehensive car rental functionality including availability search, branch management, customer management, and reservation booking.

## Core Features

- **Availability Search**: Real-time car availability with advanced filtering (branch, dates, category, transmission, fuel type, seats, price)
- **Branch Management**: Paginated listing and management of rental locations
- **Car Catalog**: Searchable car inventory with detailed specifications and filtering
- **Customer Management**: Customer registration, profiles, and management (in development)
- **Reservation System**: Complete booking flow with multi-step wizard interface
- **Maintenance Tracking**: Vehicle maintenance scheduling and tracking (planned)

## Business Domain

The system serves car rental businesses with multiple branches, managing fleets of vehicles across different categories (economy, compact, SUV, etc.) with various transmission types and fuel options. The booking flow supports date-based availability checking and customer selection processes.

## Target Users

- **Rental Agents**: Primary users managing day-to-day operations
- **Customers**: Self-service booking and account management
- **Managers**: Fleet and branch oversight
- **Maintenance Staff**: Vehicle maintenance tracking
